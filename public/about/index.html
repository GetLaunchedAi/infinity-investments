About Infinity

$10M+ in Transactions. 25+ Years of Cumulative Local Experience.

Having lived in Southwest Michigan for over 30 years, our founder brings both personal roots and professional expertise to the market. With more than 5 years of full-time building and investing experience—and over 25 years of cumulative industry knowledge—Infinity Investment Property Solutions operates as a boutique real estate investment firm tailored to individual investors.

We have successfully completed numerous flips and new builds, while leveraging a deep understanding of sales and marketing to deliver consistent, high-quality opportunities. By living and working in the communities we invest in, including Southwest Michigan and Northern Indiana, we take pride in improving neighborhoods, providing quality homes, and driving long-term value for both our investors and the community.

Our smaller size allows us to stay agile and attentive, offering personalized investment strategies that compete with—and often outperform—larger institutions.

"We treat your money like our own—because your success is our success."
